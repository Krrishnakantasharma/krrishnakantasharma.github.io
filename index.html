Android Device Utility

HTG Game Turbo is an Android utility app that shows real-time device stats, works as a game launcher, and lets you adjust standard Android settings — all in one place.

Get it on Google Play See HTG Pro
What it does

Features

Every feature works on your own device. The app shows information and lets you change your own Android settings — it does not modify games or guarantee performance results.

📊

Device Stats Monitor

Real-time RAM, storage, battery, network and hardware details in one dashboard.

🎮

Game Launcher

Add your installed games and launch them from a single organized hub.

📶

FPS & Ping Monitor

View live frame-rate and network latency information while you play.

⚙️

System Tweaks

Adjust standard Android settings such as animation scale and display density, using the free Shizuku service. Every change is reversible.

🧩

Optimization Modules

Apply bundles of predefined Android settings through a simple, guided flasher.

🔒

Privacy First

No account required, no personal data collected. All device stats stay on your device.
