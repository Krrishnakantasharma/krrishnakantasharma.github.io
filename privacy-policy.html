Privacy Policy

HTG GameZone · Last updated: April 21, 2026 · Effective: April 21, 2026

Hi Tech Gamerz ("we", "our", "us") operates the HTG GameZone Android application ("the App"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and what choices you have.

Short version: We don't collect, store, or sell your personal data. All device stats are computed on your phone and never leave it. Third-party services (AdMob) may collect limited data for ads. You can opt out anytime.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Data We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit:

  • Your name, email, phone number, or any personal identifier
  • Your location (precise or approximate)
  • Your contacts, photos, messages, or files
  • Your browsing or search history
  • Any biometric data
  • Any account credentials or passwords

1.2 Data Processed On-Device Only

The App reads the following information from your device to display it to you. This data is processed locally on your device and is never transmitted to our servers (we do not operate servers):

  • Device specifications: Model name, Android version, CPU architecture, screen resolution, manufacturer
  • Memory (RAM) usage: Total, used, and available RAM
  • Storage usage: Total, used, and free storage space
  • Battery status: Level, temperature, voltage, health, charging status
  • Network information: Connection type (WiFi/Mobile), link speed, public IP (fetched only when you tap "Check Network"), ping latency
  • Installed apps list: Package names and app icons of apps installed on your device. Used only to populate the Game Launcher list and App Manager. This list is never transmitted off your device.

1.3 Data Collected by Third Parties (AdMob)

The App displays ads via Google AdMob. Google may collect the following through AdMob SDK:

  • Advertising ID (AAID): A resettable identifier you can control in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads. Used for personalized ads.
  • Device information: Device model, OS version, general location (derived from IP, country-level), and ad interaction data
  • Crash reports and performance data to improve the ad SDK

Google's handling of this data is governed by their own Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

1.4 Data Stored Locally on Your Device

The App uses Android SharedPreferences to save your settings and preferences locally on your device only:

  • Your toggle states (Gaming Mode, DND, Screen-On, etc.)
  • Your engagement points balance and usage counters
  • Your Game Launcher list (apps you've added)
  • Your notification preferences

This data never leaves your device. Uninstalling the App removes all locally stored data.

2. Permissions We Use

The App requests the following Android permissions. Each has a clear purpose tied to a specific feature:

INTERNET / ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE

Required to display ads via AdMob and fetch public IP in the optional Network Check feature.

AD_ID

Required by Google AdMob SDK for ad attribution. You can reset or disable this in Android Settings.

POST_NOTIFICATIONS

To show you optional reminders about device stats. You can disable notifications anytime in App Settings.

ACCESS_NOTIFICATION_POLICY

Required for the Do Not Disturb (DND) toggle in Game Launcher. Only activates DND when you explicitly enable the toggle.

WRITE_SETTINGS

Required for the Rotation Lock toggle in Game Launcher. Only modifies system rotation setting when you explicitly enable the toggle. Any changes are fully reversible by toggling it off.

SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW (Display over other apps)

Required for the optional Game Toolbox floating overlay. Only activates when you explicitly enable it. You can revoke this permission anytime.

FOREGROUND_SERVICE / FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USE

Required to run the Game Toolbox floating overlay while gaming. Service runs only when you explicitly enable the toolbox.

QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES

Required to list installed apps in the Game Launcher and App Manager features. The list is used only to populate in-app UI and is never transmitted off your device.

REQUEST_DELETE_PACKAGES

Required for the App Manager feature to let you uninstall apps through the standard Android uninstall prompt. Only activates when you tap uninstall.

RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED

Required to schedule optional reminder notifications after device reboot.

3. Shizuku-Based Advanced Tweaks

The App includes optional "Advanced Tweaks" that modify Android system settings via the Shizuku service. You must install and set up Shizuku separately — the App does not grant itself any elevated permissions.

  • All tweaks require your explicit tap to apply
  • A confirmation dialog clearly explains what each tweak changes
  • All tweaks are fully reversible via the "Reset All" button
  • The App does not collect or transmit data about tweaks you apply
  • If Shizuku is not installed, this section of the App does not function — no data is collected

4. How We Use Information

Because the App does not collect personal data, we do not use your personal data for any purpose. Locally-processed device stats are shown only to you within the App. AdMob uses advertising data to serve ads — you can opt out of personalized ads in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share any personal data with third parties because we don't collect any. The only third-party involved is Google (AdMob) for serving ads, governed by Google's own Privacy Policy.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

All local data remains on your device until you:

  • Clear the App's data in Android Settings → Apps → HTG GameZone → Storage → Clear Data
  • Uninstall the App

We do not retain any copies because we do not collect data server-side.

To opt out of Google AdMob data collection, go to Android Settings → Privacy → Ads → Delete advertising ID (or reset it).

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has used the App, contact us — though we note again that no personal data is collected.

8. Security

Because all data stays on your device, the primary security boundary is your device itself. We recommend you:

  • Keep your device's screen lock enabled
  • Install OS updates regularly
  • Only install Shizuku from its official source if you use advanced tweaks

9. Changes to This Policy

If we update this policy, we will change the "Last updated" date at the top. Significant changes will be communicated via an in-App notice or update description on Google Play.

10. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under GDPR, CCPA, or similar laws to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of your personal data. Because we do not collect personal data, these rights do not typically apply. If you have questions, contact us at the address below.
