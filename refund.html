Because purchases are processed by Google Play, refund requests are handled through Google:

HTG Pro is a digital product that unlocks access immediately upon purchase. Refunds are provided in line with Google Play's policies and applicable consumer law. If a purchase was made in error or you experienced a technical problem, contact us and we will help.
